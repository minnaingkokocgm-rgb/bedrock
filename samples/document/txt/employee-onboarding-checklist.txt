Employee Onboarding Checklist
=============================

Prepared for: New Hire Orientation
Effective date: 2026-07-24

1. Complete HR paperwork (ID, tax forms, banking details).
2. Attend security awareness training within the first week.
3. Set up workstation, email, and required software accounts.
4. Meet your manager and review 30/60/90-day goals.
5. Confirm emergency contact information in the HR portal.

This document is intended for internal onboarding use only.
Please submit questions to hr@example.com.
